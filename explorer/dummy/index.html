Model assumptions. Prices are constant (2014-2016 international dollars). A crop's value per hectare and its year-to-year variability are assumed unchanged when its area changes. Each crop's area share is bounded to 0.5 to 1.5 times its current share and to within 10 percentage points of it. Only crops the country already grows are included, and area outside these crops is not modelled. Risk is the standard deviation across years of the relative shortfall of total value per hectare. The drought figure is the relative change in total value in a year with SPEI-12 = -1.5 compared with a year with SPEI-12 = 0, from each crop's drought response slope; it is shown as n/a if any crop with a non-zero share has no slope.

Phase 4: Does Crop Diversity Cause Drought Resilience Within Indian Districts?

Phases 1–3 compare 107 countries cross-sectionally, where crop diversity (hill1) is nearly time-invariant. Phase 4 tests the mechanism within Indian districts over time using 5-year lagged crop diversity (hill1_tv, years t−5..t−1) with district (αd) and state×year (λs,t) fixed effects:
