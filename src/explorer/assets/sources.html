The national tables combine FAOSTAT harvested area, production, value, food balances, producer prices and trade with MapSPAM 2020 v2r2 crop footprints and SPEIbase v2.10 drought indices. Production values are in constant 2014–2016 international dollars. Some observations carry FAOSTAT estimation or imputation flags. Unpriced production and unavailable yield anomalies have missing values.

Temperature comes from CRU TS v4.10, crop calendars from GGCMI Phase 3, national boundaries from Natural Earth, and GDP and fertilizer consumption from the World Development Indicators release of 11 November 2026.

India's core panel uses the Directorate of Economics and Statistics district records republished by the India Data Portal for 1998–2022. The five-crop 1993–2015 panel and irrigation controls use the unapportioned ICRISAT mirror. The original version-one workbook was verified against its published SHA-256 before cleaning. Historical district codes are matched to 2021 geoBoundaries polygons. Boundaries are approximate and crop areas with incomplete records are excluded from the corresponding diversity window. Rice value uses a paddy-equivalent price and a 0.67 milling factor. Unverified irrigation shares above one are excluded.

Charts show observed or derived data for the indicated sample. A gap or “n/a” indicates an unavailable observation. P-values and other statistics retain source precision in the embedded tables. Crop-response slopes require a matching crop exposure. The separate exposure-diversity analysis reports the share that uses national SPEI in the fallback_share column. Map outlines are simplified for display. Portfolio simulations are conditional scenarios. Regression coefficients describe associations.
